If Commander opens at a Not logged in prompt, enter login and provide your Keeper account email when asked.

If Commander shows an SSO Login URL and a Selection: prompt:

  1. Type o and press Enter to open the sign-in page. If that option is unavailable or the browser does not open, type c to copy the SSO Login URL and paste it into your browser's address bar. If copying fails, select and copy the URL displayed in the terminal manually.
  2. Complete your organization's sign-in and MFA in the browser.
  3. On the resulting Keeper SSO page, click Copy login token.
  4. Return to the Commander terminal, type p, and press Enter to paste the token from the clipboard. If Commander displays Paste SSO Token:, paste it there and press Enter. You can also paste the token directly at Commander's Selection: prompt.
  5. If Commander asks for two-factor authentication, choose your method from its menu. For WebAuthN (FIDO2 Security Key), follow the prompt and touch the flashing key. Menu numbers may vary.
  6. If Commander requests SSO device approval, choose Keeper Push to approve from an existing signed-in Keeper device, or Admin Approval to request approval from your administrator. Once approved, use the displayed Resume option (r) to continue. This approval happens before the device-registration command in step 3.
  7. Wait for Commander to confirm successful authentication and display its vault prompt. Completing the browser sign-in alone does not finish the Commander login.

If your account does not use SSO, follow Commander's login and MFA prompts instead.

Keep the SSO Login URL and login token private. Paste the token only into Keeper Commander. Never put tokens, passwords, or MFA codes into BOREAL, support messages, or screenshots.
